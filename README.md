# The Lights Are Still On

A 2D narrative horror platformer about a child, a broken lantern, and a town trapped in eternal night.

---

## Overview

**The Lights Are Still On** is a story-driven 2D horror game focused on exploration, atmosphere, and emotional choice rather than traditional combat.

You play as a young boy who travels to an abandoned town after receiving a letter from his grandfather. When he arrives, time feels wrong: it is always night, the lights are always on, and no one is left.

The town is not empty.
It is waiting.

---

## Core Experience

* Explore a deserted town frozen in perpetual dusk
* Use a mysterious lantern to reveal hidden truths
* Face ethereal entities that can harm and kill you
* Make meaningful choices without dialogue trees
* Uncover the fate of your family

The game emphasizes **tension, restraint, and consequence** over action-heavy combat.

---

## The Lantern

The lantern is the player’s only tool.

It has two light spectrums:

* **Normal Light**
  Shows the town as it wants to be remembered. Safe, warm, and incomplete.

* **Spectral Light**
  Reveals the ethereal realm, hidden entities, and the truth behind the town.

Using the spectral light is dangerous. Prolonged use causes instability, visual distortion, and attracts hostile entities.

The lantern may flicker or fail at critical moments.

---

## Gameplay Pillars

* 2D exploration and platforming
* Lantern-based interaction (no traditional weapons)
* Forced and optional confrontations
* Enemies that physically harm the player
* Failure and consequence without explicit morality systems

---

## Difficulty Modes

Difficulty affects **risk**, not content.

* **Easy** – Spectral light limited by time
* **Medium** – Spectral light limited by danger
* **Nightmare** – Unlimited spectral light, but psychologically punishing

All modes share the same story and encounters.

---

## Story Themes

* Truth vs comfort
* Fear of seeing too much
* Family, guilt, and responsibility
* Not everything can be saved

The story is delivered through environment, short text lines, and player action.
