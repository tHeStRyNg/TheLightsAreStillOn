# LEVEL_01.md

## Level 01 — Arrival & Awakening

This document defines the **first playable level** of *The Lights Are Still On*. It is designed as a **vertical slice foundation** and must remain tightly scoped. Everything introduced here teaches rules that will be reused throughout the game.

---

## Level Goals

* Establish atmosphere and tone
* Teach basic movement and exploration
* Introduce the town’s time anomaly
* Introduce the lantern safely
* Build dread before explicit horror
* End with the **first forced confrontation**

---

## Level Runtime Target

* **15–20 minutes** (first-time playthrough)

---

## SECTION 1 — Town Entrance

**Setting:** Edge of town / road

### Visuals

* Dusk-colored sky
* Long, unnatural shadows
* Streetlights already on
* No NPCs

### Gameplay

* Basic movement only
* No lantern yet
* Player can walk forward and backward

### Narrative Beat

* The boy arrives at 1:00 PM
* Clock confirms daytime
* Visual contradiction creates unease

### Player Learning

* Movement
* Tone: something is wrong, but not hostile

---

## SECTION 2 — Main Street

**Setting:** Central street with shops and homes

### Visuals

* All windows lit
* Interiors visible but empty
* Soft electrical hum

### Gameplay

* Exploration
* Interact with doors (locked)
* Attempt to leave town loops player back

### Narrative Beat

* Town is sealed
* No escape

### Player Learning

* World logic is broken
* Curiosity is encouraged

---

## SECTION 3 — Grandfather’s House (Exterior)

**Setting:** Old family home

### Visuals

* Warm interior light
* Familiar silhouette
* Contrast with empty street

### Gameplay

* Enterable location
* No danger

### Narrative Beat

* Emotional anchor
* Sense of safety

---

## SECTION 4 — Grandfather’s House (Interior)

**Setting:** Living room

### Visuals

* Family portraits
* Furniture untouched
* Lantern on table

### Gameplay

* Interaction prompts
* Player picks up lantern

### Narrative Beat

* Read grandfather’s note
* Rules hinted, not explained

### Player Learning

* Lantern exists
* Light matters

---

## SECTION 5 — Lantern Tutorial (Safe Space)

**Setting:** House interior

### Gameplay

* Toggle lantern spectrum
* Observe visual change
* No enemies

### Rules Introduced

* Normal light vs spectral light
* Spectral light reveals distortions

### Player Learning

* Lantern control
* Visual language

---

## SECTION 6 — The Sky Is Wrong

**Setting:** Outside the house

### Visuals

* Frozen sun
* Shadows stretch when lantern is used

### Gameplay

* Optional spectral usage
* No threats

### Narrative Beat

* Reinforces time anomaly

---

## SECTION 7 — First Watchers

**Setting:** Narrow street / alley

### Visuals

* Watchers appear only in spectral mode
* Glowing eyes in darkness

### Gameplay

* Optional encounter
* Watchers do not attack

### Player Learning

* Looking reveals danger
* Not all threats are physical

---

## SECTION 8 — Instability Introduction

**Setting:** Same area

### Gameplay

* Lantern flickers if spectral used too long
* Audio distortion

### Player Learning

* Spectral light has limits
* Risk escalates naturally

---

## SECTION 9 — Forced Confrontation Setup

**Setting:** Interior room (shop or house)

### Trigger

* Player enters room
* Doors seal

### Audio

* Music escalates

---

## SECTION 10 — First Forced Confrontation

**Enemy:** Stalker

### Gameplay Loop

1. Demon initially invisible
2. Player must use spectral light
3. Demon revealed
4. Demon approaches slowly
5. Contact causes damage

### Player Options

* Reveal and weaken
* Displace and escape
* Fail and respawn

---

## SECTION 11 — Resolution & Exit

### Outcomes

* Demon bound or avoided
* Door unlocks

### Visuals

* Room feels altered
* Silence

### Narrative Beat

* Player understands stakes

---

## Level Completion Criteria

* Lantern obtained
* First demon encountered
* Player survives at least one confrontation

---

## Scope Guardrails

* No additional enemies
* No upgrades
* No branching paths

---

## Summary

Level 01 teaches:

* Movement
* Exploration
* Lantern mechanics
* Risk and consequence

Nothing else.
