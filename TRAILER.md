# TRAILER.md

## The Lights Are Still On — Trailer Breakdown

This document defines the **official trailer structure** for *The Lights Are Still On*. It is intended to guide editing, capture planning, and music timing for a 60–90 second announcement or Steam page trailer.

---

## Trailer Goals

* Establish mood and identity within the first 5 seconds
* Clearly communicate the core premise
* Showcase the lantern mechanic visually
* Deliver one strong emotional beat
* End with a memorable title moment

The trailer should **not explain everything**. It should invite curiosity.

---

## Recommended Length

* **Primary trailer:** 60–75 seconds
* **Short cut (social):** 20–30 seconds

---

## Music Structure (3 Layers)

1. **Ambient / Lonely** — soft, distant, almost comforting
2. **Rising Tension** — introduced during spectral light usage
3. **Confrontation / Crescendo** — dramatic, emotional, overwhelming

Music escalation mirrors player choice.

## Capture Guidelines

* Avoid UI unless necessary
* Favor slow camera movement
* Let moments breathe
* Use silence as contrast

---

## What Not to Show

* Tutorials
* Menus
* Explanations of mechanics
* All demon types
* Endings

---

## Notes

This trailer is designed to:

* Work before the game is finished
* Be achievable with a vertical slice
* Support Steam page visibility
* Encourage wishlists through mood and mystery
